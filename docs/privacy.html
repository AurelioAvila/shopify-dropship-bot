Privacy Policy

Dropship Promo Uploader is an internal tool used by our team to publish short promotional videos for our own e-commerce brands, Groomlyco and Magdock, to our own TikTok accounts.

The tool accesses only the TikTok Content Posting API scopes user.info.basic (to confirm which account is connected) and video.publish (to upload a video to that account). It does not collect, store, or share any data belonging to TikTok end users or third parties. No end-user personal data is processed by this integration.
